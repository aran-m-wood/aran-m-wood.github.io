Grid A1 · Background

From reading people to reading logs

I came to security through behavior. A master's in Criminology, Law & Society taught me to ask why someone acted before asking how. That question shapes how I study threat actors and the incidents they leave behind.

My criminology capstone examined white-collar crime in the crypto industry, from why weak oversight invites it to the policies that could curb it. I continue that work through Nodo o Kiru, my self-directed practice for studying fraud typologies, OSINT methods, and blockchain tracing.
